# Getting started

The dashboard is where your team watches agents, answers approvals and looks into incidents. This page covers signing in and finding your way around.

## Sign in

There are no passwords. Sign in with your GitHub account, or with a one-time code sent to your email.

![The sign-in page](/images/guides/sign-in.webp)

1. **Continue with GitHub.** Signs you in with your GitHub account.
2. **Email.** Your email address.
3. **Email me a code.** Sends a code to that address. Enter it on the next screen to sign in. If it doesn't arrive, choose **Send a new code**.

Any verified email address or GitHub account can sign in. You stay signed in for 7 days, or until you sign out.

There are no roles to set up: anyone who signs in can use every page, as the [Accounts](/guides/settings#accounts) tab says. Every approval records who answered it.

## Find your way around

![The sidebar](/images/guides/shell.webp)

1. **Main pages.** Overview, Runs, Approvals, Incidents, Agents, Summary and Search. The page you are on has a green label. Approvals shows a count while requests are waiting.
2. **Settings.** Agent keys, accounts, retention, and the rules your code reports.
3. **Open approvals.** A shortcut to the Approvals page. While requests wait, it says how many, such as **Review 2 approvals**.
4. **Your account.** Who you are signed in as. It opens settings.
5. **Sign out.** Signs you out of the dashboard.

On small screens, the sidebar folds into a menu button at the top of the page.

## A new dashboard

Until your agents send their first events, each page shows only its heading and one short line, such as **No activity yet** on the overview or **No runs yet** on Runs. Charts, tables and filters appear as soon as there is something to show.

To connect an agent, create an [agent key](/guides/settings#agent-keys) in Settings and pass it to the SDK.

## Reading the screens

A few patterns repeat on every page. For any word you don't know, see the [glossary](/glossary).

- **Origin chips.** A small tag such as `web:supplier-portal.example untrusted` says where content came from. Untrusted origins have a warm color. Hover a chip to see its origin, trust and sensitivity.
- **Status squares.** A small square before a word shows state: green for running, amber for waiting on a person, red for blocked or failed, and grey for done or idle.
- **Clickable rows.** A row that opens something ends in a chevron and lights up on hover. Click anywhere on it.
- **Charts.** Every chart has a **Table** button that shows the same numbers as a table. Hover a chart, or move through it with the arrow keys, to read exact values.
- **The drawer.** Details open in a panel on the right. Press Escape or the close button to go back.
- **Hints.** When a button does more than its label says, hover or focus it to see a short hint.
- **Masked values.** IBANs, card numbers and emails are shown masked, such as `DE89…3000`. Full values show only on an open approval request, to the person answering it.
- **Links you can share.** Filters, searches and open steps are part of the page address. Copy the link to share exactly what you see.

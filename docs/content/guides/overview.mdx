# Overview

The overview is the home page. It shows how your agents are doing right now and what needs your attention.

Before your agents send their first run, the overview shows only its greeting and **No activity yet**. After that, any part with nothing in its time window says so in one line, such as **No runs in the last 24 hours**.

## Activity

![The top of the overview](/images/guides/overview-top.webp)

1. **Model calls.** Model calls across all agents over the last 24 hours, in 10-minute columns that end at the next 10-minute mark. The greeting at the top says how many agents are running. The page shows the numbers from when it loaded; reload it to see newer calls.
2. **Runs.** Runs started in the last 24 hours, and how many started in each hour.
3. **Guarded tools.** How many of the tools your agents used in the last 24 hours are wrapped with a guard. A tool without a guard can only be recorded, never stopped.
4. **Block rate.** The share of guarded tool calls that were blocked, per day over the last 30 days. A day above the dashed line is worth a look.
5. **Guard decisions.** Calls blocked, and calls that asked a person, in the last 24 hours.

## What needs attention

![The lists on the overview](/images/guides/overview-lists.webp)

1. **Approvals waiting.** Calls waiting for a person. **Influenced by** shows the untrusted origin that shaped a call. **No longer waiting** means the process that asked has stopped, but the request is still open. Click a row to answer it.
2. **Recent runs.** The latest runs, with their status, a strip of their guard decisions and their cost. A run with no guard decisions shows **None yet**. Click a row to open the run.
3. **Incidents.** The latest incidents, with their cause and the state of their replay.
4. **Decision log.** The latest 12 guard decisions of the last 24 hours, newest at the bottom: the time, the guard and its decision, the agent, the tool and the reason. The routine allows that permission and limit guards give every call are left out.
5. **Agents and guards.** The agents seen in the last 30 days, running ones first, each marked running or idle, and how many decisions each kind of guard made in the last 24 hours.

**View all** beside a list opens its full page. A list with nothing in it shows one line instead, such as **No approvals waiting**.

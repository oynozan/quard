# Design System: Quard

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Quiet Terminal"**

The dashboard reads like a well-kept terminal. Near-black planes sit one shade apart, 1px hairlines divide rows and panes, titles are thin, and every number, identifier and code is set in a monospace face with tabular figures. Nothing on screen is decoration or reassurance. The interface never sells itself: a label that repeats what is already visible is removed, not reworded.

Color is almost absent. Surfaces and text live on one warm black-to-off-white ramp with a faint yellow cast, never blue-grey. One green, Signal Green, marks what is on, focused, selected, in progress or the next action. A softer mint confirms and supports. Hue appears anywhere else only when time is running out or a task is unfinished. The product's character comes from a few precise details instead of color: charts drawn as fields of square cells, square "terminal" panes, a pointer-reactive dot grid behind banners, and one liquid-metal button.

This is a dense, desktop-first tool surface (Operate mode): 14px body text, 13px tables, 64px rows, a fixed left sidebar, and one right-hand drawer for every secondary flow. Motion is opacity and color at 160ms, with a short list of named exceptions. The system rejects crypto neon, glows, glassmorphism, gradient fills, mascot illustrations and drop shadows.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Warm near-black planes (#161616 up to #333333). Controls, chips and cards are tonal fills, never outlines; 1px hairlines only divide rows and panes. No shadows anywhere except the liquid-metal button.
- One signal color. Green marks state and action. It is never a surface, never body text and never a glow.
- Manrope for everything: thin (200 and 300) for titles and classifiers, 400 to 500 for anything you read or press.
- Ubuntu Mono with tabular figures for every identifier, count, code, amount and axis.
- Charts are the signature: every chart is a field of square cells with 2px gaps, lit in green like a terminal monitor, led by a 24-hour activity chart at the top of the overview.
- Square, zero-gap terminal panes for summaries, with cell meters and cell sparklines.
- One texture: a dot grid behind banners that answers the pointer.
- Loading shows grey bars sized like the value. A value that can never be known shows an em dash.
- Errors are quiet neutral boxes. Amber means "a task is waiting", not "something broke".

## Colors

A warm near-black ramp, two greens for signals, and a small set of status hues kept for time pressure and unfinished work.

### Primary

- **Signal Green** (#00DA71): The one signal color. It is used for the focus ring (2px solid, 4px offset), the fill of the primary button, checked and indeterminate checkboxes, the main series of every chart, the hero chart's live cursor, lit cells in every meter, "on" status squares, loading dots, the selected sidebar item (label and icon), the check mark of the chosen option in a menu, the spotlight ring of the product tour, the skip link fill, the accent of third-party modals, and the rim of the liquid-metal button. Contrast: 9.71:1 on #161616, 9.14:1 on #1c1c1c and 7.7:1 on #2a2a2a.

### Secondary

- **Soft Mint** (#5DDA9D): The supporting green. It is used for the hover state of the primary button, the text-selection background (with #161616 text), success states ("Copied", the "plenty of time" state of a countdown, the copied flash in a word grid), the hover state of any element whose resting text is green, and the pointer glow of the dot grid. Contrast: 10.32:1 on #161616. It is never a second chart series next to Signal Green: the two measure only 7.0 apart (ΔE in OKLab, the floor is 15), so readers cannot tell them apart.
- **Mint Wash** (#25372e): Soft Mint at about 14% over the surface (#1c1c1c). It is the fill of a short success flash. Mint text on it reaches 7.2:1.

### Status

- **Amber** (#d6b868): "A task is waiting." It is the 2px left rule and warning glyph of the unfinished-task callout and inline cautions, and the middle state of a countdown.
- **Red** (#d97878): The last third of a countdown, and failed runs inside charts.
- **Problem** (#d98c7a): An inline validation sentence under a field.
- **Caution trio** (#241c19 fill, #4a3a33 border, #e0b5a6 text): The one tinted box, placed before an irreversible action. The danger button is the caution border color at 55% as a fill.
- Success uses Soft Mint. There is no separate success green.

### Chart colors

Color in a chart is assigned by the job it does, and the job is usually "the main series".

- **Main series:** Signal Green. One per chart. A chart with one series uses no other hue.
- **Context Gray** (#747471): history, comparison series, "Other" and healthy shares. 3.86:1 on the page.
- **Chart Field** (#1c1c1c): unlit cells, the chart's grid. Gridlines are Chart Grid (#292929).
- **Heat ramp** (magnitude, one hue): Heat 1 (#2c5538), Heat 2 (#307446), Heat 3 (#2f9555), Heat 4 (#26b763), Heat 5 (#00DA71). Zero is the Chart Field. Lightness rises in steady steps (about 0.09 apart), and the dimmest step is 2.12:1 on the page.
- **Status in charts:** ok is Context Gray, warning is Amber, failed is Red. A status that stands alone always has a glyph and a word (a triangle for warnings, a cross for failures).
- **Categorical** (only when several series are the subject, such as five agents in one chart): chart-cat-1 (#00ab58, a darker green), chart-cat-2 (#9085e9, violet), chart-cat-3 (#bb4000, copper), chart-cat-4 (#3987e5, blue), chart-cat-5 (#b83669, magenta), always assigned in this order. The set passes the color-blind checks with room to spare (worst neighboring pair ΔE 19.2, or 27.7 for full color vision). Up to five series in stacks, bars and lines; up to three in scatter plots and small multiples; anything more folds into "Other" in Context Gray. Signal Green never shares a chart with the categorical set.
- **Diverging** (above or below a baseline, rare): three blue steps below (#374c68, #2f5d97, #1c6dc9) and three copper steps above (#654033, #904326, #bb4000), meeting at a #3a3a3a midpoint.
- **Hover:** lit cells turn Soft Mint and unlit cells Highlight (#333333). Mint on green is a subtle change (ΔE 7.0), so the highlighted column and the readout carry the signal.

### Named Rules

**The Signal Rule.** Green marks only what is on, focused, selected, in progress or the next action. It appears as rings, fills of the one primary action, 6px squares, lit chart and meter cells, and the live cursor. It is never a surface, a container border, body text, a heading or a glow. The hero chart is the only place green covers an area, and even there it is made of separate cells on a dark field.

**The Dark Ink Rule.** Text and icons on a green fill are #161616. White on Signal Green is 1.86:1 and is never allowed.

**The Quiet Failure Rule.** Interface errors are neutral boxes in the grey ramp. Hue is reserved for time pressure (amber, then red), unfinished tasks (amber), and status inside chart data (amber warnings and red failures, always with a glyph and a word). Nothing turns red because a request failed, and nothing turns green because it succeeded, beyond a mint word.

## Typography

**UI and Display Font:** Manrope, variable, weights 200 to 800 (fallback `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif`)
**Data Font:** Ubuntu Mono 400 (700 is available but unused), always with tabular figures
**Marketing Display Font:** Akt 400 and 500, for public marketing headlines only, never inside the dashboard

**Character:** Manrope carries both the quiet UI and the thin display lines, so the whole product speaks with one voice. Thin weights do the titling work that size and bold would do elsewhere. Ubuntu Mono makes every value look like something read from a terminal: exact, aligned, countable.

Load all three with `next/font/google` (`Manrope` with `variable: "--font-manrope"` and no weight list, `Ubuntu_Mono` with weights 400 and 700, `Akt` with weights 400 and 500), and map `--font-sans` and `--font-heading` to Manrope and `--font-mono` to Ubuntu Mono in the Tailwind theme. This replaces the Geist font that the shadcn setup added. The root element gets grayscale font smoothing (Tailwind `antialiased`). The body is Manrope 14px, line-height 1.55, `font-kerning: normal` and `text-rendering: optimizeLegibility`.

### Manrope settings for the light roles

These titles and classifiers were drawn in a wide face at weight 200. Manrope is narrower and its 200 is fainter at small sizes, so two settings change. Both were checked in side-by-side renders on #161616.

- Text at 15px and above keeps weight 200. Text at 11px to 13px uses weight 300, which matches the apparent weight of the original at those sizes. At 200, Manrope labels at 11px fade out on the dark background.
- Display lines at 26px to 27px use -0.2px tracking instead of -0.5px and -0.7px. Tighter tracking crowds Manrope's letters.

### Text conventions

- Sentence case everywhere. Only the sidebar caption and the mono eyebrow are uppercase, and they get it from CSS.
- A button that is working replaces its label with the step in progress, ending in a single ellipsis character ("Encrypting…", "Waiting for confirmation…"). Never three dots.
- Loading is a grey bar. A value that cannot be known is an em dash (—). An absent value is a plain word in the Absent color ("Only you", "Not set", "None").
- Counters in terminal cards are zero-padded to two digits. Fractions are written with spaces, "12 / 30". Step counters are "1 / 4" in a wizard and "01 / 05" in a tour.
- A figure always carries its unit. Dates are short en-GB in UTC ("3 Oct 2026"), with the long form ("3 October 2026 at 14:05") in a title tooltip.
- Errors are full sentences: what happened, then what to do. The fallback is "Something went wrong. Try again."
- Warnings come before the action they qualify, never after it.

### Named Rules

**The Mono Truth Rule.** Anything that is an identifier, a count, a code, an amount, a fraction or an axis value is Ubuntu Mono with tabular figures. Words are Manrope. A mono class must never lose to a heading rule; set the family explicitly on figures.

**The Light Voice Rule.** Titles and classifiers are thin (200 or 300) and never bold. Weight rises only for things you read closely or press: 450 for buttons and row names, 500 for the primary button and small headings.

### Named Rules

**The Shared Edge Rule.** Tables that stack on one page share column geometry, so their first columns, icon tiles and right edges line up to within 0.5px. Every table plans for a 44px selection column, real or merged into the first cell.

**The Named Once Rule.** Every piece of information appears once. The sidebar names the page on desktop, so no top bar repeats it. A drawer's title repeats the label of the control that opened it, and nothing else does.

**The One Line Rule.** Pages do not explain themselves. A heading gets at most one short line under it, and only when the page cannot be read without it. There are no info icons: an explanation that belongs to a control appears as a hint while that control is hovered or focused (the Hint: a #2b2b2b popover, 12px Manrope 300, 300ms delay, no border, no icon), and most other explanations are simply cut. A repeated fact becomes a two-word marker ("model-generated"). A card or row shows a name, one meta line and a status; anything more is one click away.

**The Two Cues Rule.** Everything clickable shows a cue at rest and a second one on hover, and nothing else gets either. Inline links are always underlined (Text Link, Hover Hairline underline, Text Bright on hover). Links to another view end in a chevron ("View all ›"). A row that opens something is clickable across its whole width, ends in a Faint chevron, and on hover fills with Nav Hover, brightens the chevron and shows a pointer. Rows that do not open anything have no hover fill. Buttons keep their fill. Clickable chart marks and graph nodes take a pointer and the hover ring.

**The Exceptions First Rule.** Lists, checks and paths lead with what needs a person: asks, blocks, would-blocks, untrusted values and failures. Healthy items fold into one muted count ("2 more passed") that opens in place. A fact that is already visible on the page is not repeated in another block, and colors that a chip or a word already names get no legend.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Depth comes from tonal steps (#161616 for the page, #181818 for the rail, #191919 for the drawer, #1c1c1c for surfaces, #242424 for hover fills, #2a2a2a for selection) and from 1px hairlines. No card, menu, toast, drawer or modal has a shadow, including third-party ones, which get `box-shadow: none` and a 1px Strong Hairline instead.

Overlays darken instead of lifting: the drawer and the mobile sidebar sit over a Scrim (black at 60%), and the product tour dims the page with black at 65% opacity. Nothing is frosted or blurred: every dashboard page needs a session, so there is no signed-out version of a page.

### Shadow Vocabulary

Only the liquid-metal button casts shadows, because it is the one physical object in the interface.

- **Metal at rest** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,0.3), 0 36px 14px 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.02), 0 20px 12px 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.08), 0 9px 9px 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.12), 0 2px 5px 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.15)`).
- **Metal on hover** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,0.4), 0 12px 6px 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.05), 0 8px 5px 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.1), 0 4px 4px 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.15), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.2)`). The button tightens toward the page.
- **Metal pressed** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,0.5), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.3)` on the frame, plus `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.4), inset 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.3)` on the plate).
- **Metal label** (`text-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`).
- Shadow changes take 150ms with `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`.

### Named Rules

**The Hairline Rule.** Borders, never shadows. If a surface needs separating, it gets a 1px line or one tonal step. The liquid-metal button is the single exception.

**The No Outline Rule.** Controls, chips, tiles and cards are tonal fills, never outlines. Secondary buttons and select triggers are #2b2b2b (#333 on hover), the destructive button is a warm fill (Caution Border at 55%, full on hover), fields are #222, and chips, badges and counts are #252525 (untrusted chips keep the warm #241c19). A card or tile inside a pane is set apart by a fill step or by spacing, never by a box. Hairlines remain only where they divide: table and list rows, the line under a pane header, and the edges of terminal panes. Focus still draws the green ring.

## Shapes

The form language is small, even radii on everything you touch, square corners on everything that tiles, and one deliberate large curve.

- **6px** for containers and controls: buttons, inputs, selects, menus, nav items, banners, the summary rail, value wells, error and caution boxes, toasts, the drawer's inner elements, wizard and tour cards, and the tour spotlight cutout.
- **4px** for chips and small tiles: badges, count chips, keyboard hints, checkboxes, icon tiles, the copy chip, skeleton bars, removable chips and the inner plate of the liquid-metal button.
- **3px** only for highlighted rows inside a menu, so they sit inside the menu's 6px corner.
- **0** on purpose for terminal panes, table rows and header bands, chart and meter cells, 6px status squares, and the full-bleed sidebar, drawer and top bar.
- **50%** for circles: loading dots, the spinner, the floating tour button and the click ripple.
- **32px**, the one large curve: the hero chart's cell field has 32px corners on the top-left, top-right and bottom-right, drawn in whole cells (a cell is drawn only when its center is inside the curve), so the corners step like a terminal drawing. The bottom-left stays square because it is the chart's origin.

Borders are 1px solid everywhere. The exceptions: the 2px amber left rule of warnings, the 2px spinner ring, 1.5px checkmark strokes, and the 1px dashed swatch of a chart's second series. Tiled cards draw a shared edge once by dropping one side's border. Grids of cells draw their lines as 1px gaps over a hairline-colored background instead of giving each cell a border.

In shadcn, set `--radius: 6px` and override the derived scale so nothing grows past it: `--radius-sm: 4px` and `--radius-md`, `--radius-lg` and `--radius-xl` all 6px. The hero's 32px stepped corners, 3px menu rows and circles are set where they are used.

## Components

### Buttons

Flat tonal slabs with no border. The primary is the only bright surface in the interface.

- **Shape:** 6px radius, minimum height 34px, padding 7px 14px, a transparent 1px border that only reserves space, Manrope 14px weight 450, no wrapping, inline-flex and centered, with 7px to 10px between an icon and the label.
- **Primary:** Signal Green fill, #161616 label at weight 500. On hover the fill changes to Soft Mint. Icons inside take the label color. Use it for form submits and confirmations inside the drawer, wizard steps and the tour's Next button.
- **Secondary (default):** a flat Control Hover fill (#2b2b2b), no border, Text Soft label at weight 450. On hover the fill becomes Highlight (#333333) and the label Text Bright.
- **Small:** 13px, minimum height 30px, padding 5px 11px. Used in tables, toolbars, the tour footer and copy actions.
- **Full width:** width 100% and margin-top 26px, or 0 when it follows help text.
- **Text button:** No box and no underline. 13px Text Link, brightening to Text Bright on hover. Used for "View all", Cancel, Skip, Dismiss and quiet actions in headings.
- **Icon button (ghost):** 32×32, 6px radius, no fill and no border at rest, glyph in Text Secondary. On hover: #2b2b2b fill and glyph Text Bright. The toolbar's refresh button is one. Mini sizes are 24×24 beside a figure label and 20×20 inside a chip.
- **Danger:** a warm fill, Caution Border (#4a3a33) at 55%, with no border and a Caution Text label at weight 500. On hover the fill becomes the full Caution Border and the label Text Bright. This is an adaptation: a green fill on "Remove" would read as a positive action, so destructive confirmations use this instead of the primary.
- **States:** Focus shows the 2px Signal Green outline at a 4px offset, following the radius. Disabled is opacity 0.45 with a not-allowed cursor, and hover styles must not apply while disabled. There is no pressed movement and no `:active` style. While busy, the label becomes the step in progress ending in "…", the button is disabled, and a 13px spinner sits 9px before the label.
- **Explanation:** a button whose effect is not obvious from its label carries a Hint (see Hint), shown while it is hovered or focused. It never gets an info icon beside it.
- **Motion:** color, background-color, border-color and opacity transition over 160ms with the default ease.
- **Mobile:** at 760px and below, buttons and icon buttons get a 34px minimum height. Small buttons keep 30px.

### Liquid-metal button (signature)

A dark plate inside a living 2px chrome rim. It is the one ornamental, shadowed object, and it is used once per region for the action that region exists for.

- **Where:** only the sidebar's create action (full width). Never inside the drawer or the summary rail. Forms always use the plain primary button.
- **Geometry:** always 46px tall. 142×46 on its own, 46×46 in icon mode, full width in the sidebar slot. The outer frame has a 6px radius. The plate is inset 2px with a 4px radius, so the rim is an even 2px all round. Plate fill: `linear-gradient(21deg, #1c1c1c 0%, #212121 100%)`.
- **Label:** Manrope 14px weight 400, #fdfdfd, `text-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`, centered, 6px gap to an optional icon.
- **Layers:** a root with `isolation: isolate`, then four stacked layers: z-index 10 is the shader frame with a canvas that fills it, z-index 20 is the plate, z-index 30 is the label (`pointer-events: none`), and z-index 40 is a transparent full-size `<button>` (or `<a>` when it navigates) that holds the ripples and carries `aria-label`.
- **Shader:** `@paper-design/shaders` (0.0.81), `ShaderMount` with `liquidMetalFragmentShader` and these uniforms: `u_repetition 4`, `u_softness 0.5`, `u_shiftRed 0.3`, `u_shiftBlue 0.3`, `u_distortion 0`, `u_contour 0`, `u_angle 45`, `u_scale 8`, `u_shape` none, origin 0.5 and 0.5, offset 0 and 0. The shape must be none so the shader covers the whole canvas and CSS alone shapes the frame. Speed is 0.6 at rest, 1 on hover, and 2.4 for 300ms after a click before it returns to 1 or 0.6.
- **Green rim:** inside the shader frame, above the canvas and below the plate, place a layer filled with Signal Green and `mix-blend-mode: multiply`. The metal's highlights become Signal Green (about #00D671) and its dark bands become near-black green, so the 2px rim reads as green metal while keeping its moving light. Do not use the shader's `u_colorTint` for this. It is a color-burn blend: with Signal Green it turns the highlights cyan, and with Soft Mint it leaves them near-white. Leave `u_colorTint` and `u_colorBack` unset.
- **Shadows:** the metal shadow stacks in Elevation & Depth. They swap in 150ms on hover and press.
- **Click:** a 20px circle appears at the pointer with `radial-gradient(circle, rgba(0,218,113,0.4) 0%, rgba(0,218,113,0) 70%)`, grows from scale 0 to 4 while fading from 0.6 to 0 over 600ms ease-out, and is removed.
- **States:** disabled sets opacity 0.45 on the whole button, and a title explains what is missing ("Connect an account first"). The hit surface must show the green focus outline (the original hid it). Space and Enter show the pressed state like a mouse press does. If WebGL fails, the plate and its 1px dark ring remain, and the label and click still work.
- **Performance:** the library already pauses when the button is off-screen or the tab is hidden, and renders at twice the CSS pixel size at least. Under reduced motion, call `setSpeed(0)` and skip the ripple instead of showing a static dot.
- **Neighbors:** plain buttons in the same row are raised to a 46px minimum height. In a banner the button is `flex: 0 1 200px` with a 190px minimum width. At 560px and below it goes full width at the top of a stacked action list.

### Inputs and fields

Flat filled boxes with no border at rest. A light edge and the green ring appear only on focus, never a glow.

- **Field:** width 100%, padding 10px 12px, Manrope 14px, a transparent 1px border, 6px radius, Control fill (#222222), Foreground text, 18px below. Labels are block-level, 14px, 9px above the field.
- **Textarea:** the same box in Ubuntu Mono 13px, resizable vertically only.
- **Compact field** (inline add forms): padding 9px 11px, 13px.
- **Suffixed field:** one Control-filled box with no border holding the input and a fixed suffix (flex, gap 10px, padding 0 12px). The input is 15px with padding 11px 0 and no border. The suffix is Ubuntu Mono 15px in Muted, so the whole value reads as one string.
- **Search field:** 32px tall, Control fill, no border, padding 0 9px, gap 8px. It holds a 17px search icon at opacity 0.45, a 13px input, and a keyboard hint ("/" in Ubuntu Mono 10px, Absent color, on a Tile fill, 4px radius, padding 0 4px). It has a 360px maximum width on full pages. Pressing "/" focuses it unless focus is already in a field or a dialog is open. At 760px and below it takes its own row and the hint is hidden.
- **Placeholder:** Faint for every field.
- **Focus:** the transparent border becomes Field Focus and the 2px Signal Green ring appears at a 4px offset. Fields that wrap their input in a styled box (search, suffixed) hide the ring and turn their border Signal Green instead, so focus always shows green. At rest no field shows an edge.
- **Help text:** 12px Muted, line-height 1.8, margin 14px 0 20px.
- **Attached hint:** pulled up under its field with margin -10px 0 18px, 12px, line-height 1.7. A positive hint can show a decoded summary in Text Link with one word at weight 600. A problem hint is the Problem color.
- **Derived preview:** a live mono preview of the final identifier directly under the input. Ubuntu Mono 12px in Faint, margin -10px 0 20px.
- **Validation:** no red borders and no `aria-invalid` styling. Field problems are a sentence under the field, checked on blur rather than on every keystroke. A field that has been emptied says nothing.
- **Disclosure:** a text button with a plus drawn from two 1px bars in a 9×9 box. When open, the plus rotates 45 degrees into a cross over 220ms `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0.2, 1)`. The hidden section follows it directly, with 14px below the toggle. Never use a text caret character.

### Select

A filled trigger with no border, like the secondary button, and a flat menu that fades in and never slides.

- **Trigger:** minimum height 42px, padding 10px 12px, gap 16px, Control Hover fill (#2b2b2b), a transparent border, 6px radius, 14px text that ends with an ellipsis. A 14px chevron glyph (stroke 2.4) in Muted.
- **Compact trigger** (toolbars): maximum width 160px, minimum height 32px, padding 5px 9px, gap 12px, 12px text, the same fill.
- **States:** hover and open give a Highlight fill (#333333), over 160ms, with no border. Focus gives a 2px Signal Green outline at a 3px offset. Disabled is opacity 0.45.
- **Menu:** opens 6px below the trigger, start-aligned, at least as wide as the trigger, at most `100vw - 16px` wide, at most `min(340px, available height)` tall, and stays 8px from the viewport edges. Surface fill, 1px Strong Hairline (a floating menu keeps its edge), 6px radius, no shadow, Manrope 13px with line-height 1.5, 4px inner padding. It fades in over 120ms ease-out.

### Charts (signature)

Every chart is a field of square cells with one fixed 2px gap, like a terminal system monitor. A value lights whole cells, then lights the next cell from its bottom by the remainder, so counting cells reads the value. The faint unlit field is the chart's grid. Green lights only the signal: the main series, lit meter cells, the busiest heat step and the live cursor. History, comparison series and healthy shares stay quiet grey. Text never sits on cells. A label knocks its cells out, the way a title sits in a terminal border.

#### Foundations

- **Cell:** square, no radius, 2px gap everywhere. The cell size depends on the chart: 4px in the hero and traces, 5px in histograms and outcome bars, 6px in row sparklines, 7px to 8px in meters and tile sparklines, and 16px in heatmaps. Pitch is the cell plus the 2px gap.
- **Value rule:** a column lights floor(value ÷ unit) full cells, then lights the next cell from its bottom by the remainder, snapped to whole device pixels. Any value above zero shows at least one device pixel. Zero shows nothing. Gridlines sit on unit boundaries, so reading against the ticks stays exact.
- **Field:** unlit cells are Chart Field (#1c1c1c). They are the grid, never data. Gridlines are 1px Chart Grid (#292929) lines drawn inside the row gaps at each tick.
- **Labels:** y ticks are Mono axis in Muted, right-aligned in a 34px column and centered on their gridlines. The hero is the exception: its ticks hang in the page gutter. X labels sit 6px to 8px below the field. A label inside the field (a percentile or a breach, never a peak value) knocks out its cells: its text box plus one pitch, snapped to whole cells, usually inside a top band of rows that data never enters.
- **Pane chrome:** charts live in terminal panes. The 38px Recess header holds the title (Label, Text Link) on the left, and a mono tag ("24H", "30D"), a 1×12 hairline divider and a "Table" toggle (Manrope 12px, Text Link, underlined in Hover Hairline at a 3px offset; Text Bright with a Text Secondary underline on hover) on the right. An optional readout row sits under the header, 12px above the field: Meta label pairs in Muted with Ubuntu Mono 12px values in Foreground, 7px apart, 20px between pairs.
- **Color:** see Chart colors in the Colors section. The main series is Signal Green, history and comparison are Context Gray, magnitude uses five heat steps, and status uses amber and red with a glyph and a word.
- **Hover and focus:** bars, columns and heat cells are their own hit targets. Time series snap to the nearest column across the full plot height. The hovered column's unlit cells turn Highlight (#333333) and its lit cells Soft Mint. Its x label turns Foreground, and fixed labels nearby hide. A readout opens 8px to 14px beside the mark, on the side with more room: Control Hover fill (#2b2b2b), no border, 4px radius, padding 6px 9px 7px, no shadow and no arrow. Inside it: a 4px key cell in the mark's color, the value in Ubuntu Mono 13px Foreground and the unit in Manrope 300 12px Muted on one line (6px gaps), and the time or bucket below in Ubuntu Mono 11px Muted, indented 10px to clear the key cell. It fades in over 160ms. The field is focusable: Left and Right (plus Up and Down in heatmaps) move the hover, Home and End jump, and Escape clears. The readout is repeated in a polite live region.
- **Table view:** every chart pane has the "Table" toggle (`aria-pressed`). It swaps the field for a compact table in the same pane body: a 30px sticky Recess header (11px Muted), 28px rows, Ubuntu Mono 12px, padding 0 12px, values right-aligned. The toggle then reads "Chart".
- **Loading:** the field stays and a sweep crosses it from left to right. Unlit cells step from #1c1c1c to #222222, #2b2b2b and #2f2f2f as the sweep's center comes within 7, 4 and 2 columns, over 1.4s ease-in-out, repeating. Readouts and ticks become skeleton bars, and fixed x labels stay. Never green while loading. The pane gets `aria-busy`.
- **Empty:** the field stays, and a centered knockout sentence explains it (Manrope 11px Muted, "No model calls in the last 24 hours"). Ticks and readouts become em dashes.
- **Error:** a read that fails is never drawn inside a field. The page shows its heading and a neutral error box with "Try again" (see Feedback blocks).
- **Refetch:** the previous frame stays at 45% opacity with a 13px spinner before the tag. No skeleton flash and no layout jump.
- **Live updates:** new data redraws in place. A new column appears at the right and the window shifts by redrawing. No slide, tween or draw-in.
- **Reduced motion:** the hero cursor holds steady and the loading sweep stops. Everything else is already static.
- **Accessibility:** every field is `role="img"` with a one-sentence summary ("Model calls per 10 min over the last 24 hours. Peak 144 at 14:40, now 13."). A status also has a glyph and a word. The table view is the full equivalent of the chart.
- **Implementation:** SVG at device size with `shape-rendering: crispEdges` and integer coordinates. Use one `<path>` per color and append one rectangle subpath per cell, so thousands of cells cost a handful of DOM nodes. Build the path strings in `useMemo` from the data, the pitch and the hover. A ResizeObserver recomputes the pitch. Labels and readouts are HTML positioned over the SVG. Hover is arithmetic (column = floor((x − left) ÷ pitch)), with no listener per cell. Do not use SVG `transform` attributes. Above about 10,000 cells, or for 60fps streams, draw the same geometry on a 2D canvas scaled to the device pixel ratio.

#### Cell meter

- One row of N square cells lit from the left: lit = ratio > 0 ? max(1, round(ratio × N)) : 0, so any value above zero lights at least one cell. N is fixed per context: 24 in tiles, 22 in the summary rail and 20 in the countdown.
- Widths come from the track: base = floor((track − 2 × (N − 1)) ÷ N), and the leftover pixels go to evenly spaced cells, so the meter spans its gutter exactly. The height equals the base width: about 7px in tiles and 8px in the rail. The countdown keeps its compact 112 × 11 strip.
- Lit cells are Signal Green. Unlit cells are Segment Off (#2f2f2f), brighter than the chart field, because the remaining share is part of the reading.
- Status fills come from `--progress-fill` and are used in countdowns only: Soft Mint while more than two thirds remain, Amber at two thirds or less, Red at one third or less, changing over 180ms ease-out. Elsewhere the meter snaps.
- `role="progressbar"` with min, max and now values and a sentence label ("22 of 50 concurrency slots in use").

#### Cell trace

- For rates and other values that are not counts (error rate per day, p95 latency over time). Counts use columns.
- A field of 4px cells (pitch 6), for example 70 × 24 cells in a pane body. The line is rasterized onto cells: for each column, light every row between the lowest and highest value the line reaches across that column, so a steep change becomes a vertical run of cells.
- **Limit:** a 1px Amber dashed line in a row gap, one 4px dash per column, named in the legend ("Limit 2.00%"). Cells at or above the limit turn Amber. A breach also gets a direct label (a warning glyph with "2.75% · 25 Sep") knocked out at the top edge, so it reads without color.
- **Legend:** above the field: two cells in the series color with "Today 1.76%", and a dashed swatch with "Limit 2.00%".
- **Comparison series** (previous period, baseline): drawn in Context Gray cells. Where both series light a cell, the main series wins. Never Soft Mint.

#### Where charts depart from common chart defaults

- Square cell ends instead of rounded bar ends, and marks are cut by the 2px cell gap.
- Lines are rasterized cell traces, not 2px strokes, and markers are cells, not 8px dots.
- No area washes. A dim green already means "a little" in the heat ramp.
- Numbers stay in Ubuntu Mono at every size, including the big counters.
- Meter tracks are neutral #2f2f2f, not a dim green, so green never becomes wallpaper.
- "Ok" is quiet grey, not green. Green means "on", and green next to amber is too close for color-blind readers (ΔE 3.0).
- Reference lines (limits, percentiles) are dashed to the cell rhythm. Gridlines stay solid inside the row gaps.

### Dot grid

A quiet lattice behind banners that answers the pointer.

- A canvas filling its banner (absolute, inset 0, z-index -1, `pointer-events: none`), sized for the device pixel ratio capped at 2.
- Dots of 1px radius on a 16px grid, centered in the box: columns = floor(width / 16), rows = floor(height / 16), first x = (width − (columns − 1) × 16) / 2, and the same for y.
- Resting color Dot (#404040). Within 140px of the pointer a dot moves linearly toward Soft Mint: mix = max(0, 1 − distance / 140).
- It redraws on pointer move (one frame per move) and resets when the pointer leaves. There is no ambient animation. Under reduced motion the dots stay at rest.
- It sits behind promo banners and the figure banner, never behind a chart.

### Search results

- **Search row:** a 46px search field (Control fill, no border, padding 0 13px, gap 10px) with the query in Ubuntu Mono 15px and the placeholder in Manrope 14px, and a tall primary "Search" button (112px minimum, "Searching…" while busy), at most 860px wide, 8px apart. They stack at 760px and below. Escape clears the field. The query lives in the URL.
- **Examples:** 14px below, "Try" in 13px Muted, then example chips 6px apart: Ubuntu Mono 12px Text Secondary on a Tile fill, no border, 4px radius, padding 3px 8px. Hover gives a Highlight fill and Text Bright. The current one (`aria-current`) keeps a Selected fill and Text Bright.
- **Summary:** 30px below (25px at 760px and below). "14 matches in 3 runs" as a Section title with mono figures, then the kind as a badge. The searched value is not repeated, since the field shows it; only a masked form that differs from the query appears, in Ubuntu Mono 13px Text Secondary. A "by hash" marker (a 14px key icon and 12px Muted words) shows when a sensitive value was matched by its keyed hash, and "newest 50" in 12px Muted when results are cut. Results under a running search dim to 0.55.
- **Table:** a full-page table (39px header, 14px text, minimum width 960px). Each run opens with a group row on Panel Black (50px): "Run 7f3a…" as a 13px mono text link, its status, "Started by agent · date, time" and the match count on the right, indented by the 44px selection column. Match rows are clickable rows: the step as a mono name cell, the agent, the field in mono, the matched value in Ubuntu Mono 13px Text Soft with the match kind under it in 11px Note, the origin chip, and the time with the row chevron. ### Iconography

Two families of stroke drawings on one 48-unit grid, both with round caps and joins.

- **Pictorial icons:** Arcticons line icons, about 1 unit of stroke on the 48 grid (about 0.42px at 20px). They are GPL-3.0 licensed, so ship the license with them or draw equivalents on the same grid. Render them inline or as CSS masks so they can take `currentColor`; the selected nav icon must turn green. Sizes: 15, 17, 18, 20 (default) and 22px. Emphasis comes from opacity: 0.45 in the search field, 0.6 in notices, wells and chevrons, 0.62 in nav at rest, 0.75 in account tiles, 0.8 in row tiles, 0.85 in nav on hover, and 1 when selected.
- **Control glyphs:** inline SVG with `currentColor` at stroke-width 1.5 (2.2 for the warning glyph, 2.4 for the select chevron and check). Paths on the 48 grid: chevron right `M18 12L30 24L18 36`; close `M15 15L33 33M33 15L15 33`; refresh `M33.9 33.9A14 14 0 1 1 33.9 14.1M34.2 7.6L33.9 14.1L27.4 14.4`; copy `M17 17H35V36H17ZM11 30H9V10H28V12`; wand `M10 36L31 15L36 20L15 41ZM27 19L32 24M13 8V16M9 12H17M35 5V11M32 8H38M38 29V37M34 33H42`; warning `M24 8L43 40H5ZM24 19V29M24 34V35`; select chevron `M12 19l12 12 12-12`; check `m12 24 8 8 16-16`.
- shadcn installed Lucide. If a Lucide icon fills a gap, set `strokeWidth` to 0.75 to match the control glyphs, or 0.5 to match the pictorial icons.
- On a green fill, icons are #161616. A text character never stands in for an icon (no ▶, ×, ✓, ↻ or ☰).

### Motion

- **Default:** opacity and color. Controls transition color, background-color, border-color and opacity over 160ms with the default ease.
- **Entrances:** content fades from opacity 0 to 1 over 400ms `cubic-bezier(0.215, 0.61, 0.355, 1)`, with no movement. Overview blocks stagger by 40ms (0, 40, 80, 120 and 160ms), rail sections by 80ms, and sidebar rows by 50ms. The `motion` library handles these, and under reduced motion it renders the final state.
- **Easings:** `cubic-bezier(0.215, 0.61, 0.355, 1)` for every entrance, `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` to open the drawer, `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)` to close it, ease-out for small fades (menus 120ms, tour copy and wizard steps 160ms, segment colors 180ms), ease-in-out for the skeleton sweep, and linear for the spinner and loading dots.
- **Named exceptions** (the only motion that is not opacity or color): the logo fly-in, the drawer's clip-path wipe, the spinner's rotation, the disclosure plus rotating into a cross, the liquid-metal ripple, the skeleton's background sweep, the loading sweep across chart fields, the hero cursor's blink, and the live code's blur clearing.
- **Reduced motion:** a global rule removes CSS animations and transitions. Code checks the same preference: the hero cursor holds steady, chart loading sweeps stop, the dot grid stays at rest, the drawer skips its wipe, entrances render settled, confetti is skipped, and the liquid-metal shader stops. The spinner is the one exception and keeps turning slowly.

### Tooltips

- Most tooltips are native `title` attributes, following four rules. A truncated or summarized cell carries its full value. A short date carries the long date and time. A disabled control says why it is disabled. A shortcut appears in its control's title ("Clear selection (Esc)").
- Three tooltips are styled: the Hint below, the chart readout (see Charts) and the copy chip. Each is a neutral fill with no border (#2b2b2b, or Tile for the copy chip), a 4px radius, a 160ms opacity fade, no arrow and no shadow.

### Links and clickable rows

The link kit (`kit/links.tsx`) implements [the Two Cues Rule](#named-rules-2).

- **Text link:** an inline link, always underlined: Text Link with a Hover Hairline underline at a 3px offset. On hover the text turns Text Bright and the underline Text Secondary. A mono variant is used for run ids and agent names.
- **Arrow link:** a link to another view: 13px Text Link words, a 3px gap and a 14px chevron glyph at 0.6, no wrapping. On hover the words turn Text Bright and the chevron rises to 1. The "View all" link of a section heading is one.
- **Clickable row:** a row that opens something sets `Tr interactive` (Nav Hover fill and a pointer on hover). One cell holds a row link, whose hit area is stretched over the whole row, so the row is one link for keyboard and screen readers; its focus is a 2px Signal Green outline inset 2px. The last cell ends in a row chevron: a 16px glyph in Faint, 8px from the value, turning Text Bright while the row is hovered. Rows that open nothing use a plain `Tr`, with no fill and no chevron.

### Hint

The Hint (`ui/hint.tsx`) is how a control explains itself, as [the One Line Rule](#named-rules-2) asks. There are no info icons anywhere: the Info tip and its round (i) button are retired.

- **Trigger:** the control itself. The Hint wraps one focusable element, a button or a link, and opens while that element is hovered (after 300ms) or keyboard-focused. It adds no icon, no underline and no space to the layout. Plain text that is not focusable never gets a Hint; its fact is either a short visible marker of four words or fewer ("model-generated", "observe only", "by hash") or it is cut.
- **Popup:** 8px above the control by default (any side can be asked for), kept 12px inside the viewport. Control Hover fill (#2b2b2b), no border, 4px radius, padding 7px 10px, at most 260px wide, Manrope 300 12px Text Soft at line-height 1.5. No shadow and no arrow. It fades over 160ms. Inside the drawer it portals into the dialog so it stays above it.
- **Copy:** one short sentence that says what the control does that its label cannot ("Same agent, tool and exact arguments, in any run, until revoked."). Figures and identifiers inside it stay mono.

### Third-party interfaces

Vendor modals, tours and toasts are pulled onto this system rather than left in their own palette. Pass Surface as their background and Signal Green as their accent through their configuration, map their CSS variables onto the neutral ramp (borders to the hairlines, secondary text to Muted and Subtle, hover fills to Hover), remove their shadows and give them a 1px Strong Hairline. Remove their slide and scale transitions as well.

### Small screens and system pages

- **Skip link:** the first focusable element, fixed at left 16px and hidden above the viewport until focused, then shown at top 12px. Signal Green fill, #161616 text, padding 10px 16px, 6px radius.
- **Not found and configuration screens:** a centered column at full viewport height with a 16px gap and 24px padding: a 24px title, one Muted sentence, and two links (one secondary button, one primary).

### Stack mapping

- **Next.js 16:** App Router with React Compiler. Load fonts with `next/font/google` in the root layout and put the tokens in `globals.css`.
- **Tailwind 4:** tokens become CSS variables in `:root` and are mapped in `@theme inline`. Tailwind's base reset makes headings inherit size and weight, strips list bullets and makes form controls transparent, so every component sets its own values. Use one explicit placeholder color (Faint) instead of the reset's 50% mix.
- **Layering:** put the global element rules (button and link resets, `color: inherit`, heading margins, the 160ms transition list, the focus outline, selection) inside `@layer base`, after shadcn's base block. Unlayered element rules beat every Tailwind layer, so they would strip shadcn's button fills and borders and draw a second focus ring on top of its own.
- **shadcn (base-nova, Base UI):**
    - Button: `default` is the primary, `outline` restyled as the flat #2b2b2b fill with no border is the secondary, `ghost` covers icon and text buttons, `link` is the text button, and `destructive` is the danger button. Do not use the `secondary` variant as a button tier: Soft Mint is a signal, not a button color.
    - Remove the preset's press nudge (`translate-y-px`), and draw focus with outline utilities (2px Signal Green, 4px offset) instead of the box-shadow ring utilities.
    - Tooltip becomes the Hint (300ms delay, no icon).
    - Sheet (right side) becomes the drawer, with the clip-path wipe instead of a slide.
    - Select, Checkbox, Badge, Skeleton, Tooltip and Sonner are restyled to the specs above.
    - Progress is replaced by the segmented meter, because rounded smooth bars do not belong here.
- **Libraries:** `motion` for entrances, `@paper-design/shaders` for the liquid-metal rim, `sonner` for toasts, `canvas-confetti` for the single success burst, and a product-tour library whose card, mask and pointer are replaced by the custom ones above.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** keep every surface on the neutral ramp and let Signal Green (#00DA71) mark only state and action: focus, selection, progress, "on", and the one primary action.
- **Do** put #161616 text and icons on every green fill.
- **Do** set every identifier, count, code, amount, fraction and axis value in Ubuntu Mono with tabular figures. Zero-pad terminal counters to two digits and write fractions as "12 / 30".
- **Do** use Manrope 200 for titles at 15px and up, and Manrope 300 for classifiers at 11px to 13px.
- **Do** show a skeleton bar sized like the coming value while loading, and an em dash for a value that can never be known.
- **Do** keep stacked tables on the shared 44px selection column and 54px first-cell indent, so their columns line up to within 0.5px.
- **Do** route every secondary flow through the one 420px drawer, titled with the label of the control that opened it.
- **Do** use exactly one liquid-metal button per region, tinted with Soft Mint, and raise its neighbors to 46px.
- **Do** place warnings and caution boxes before the action they qualify.
- **Do** honor reduced motion in both CSS and code, and keep the spinner turning slowly.
- **Do** check for no horizontal page overflow at 390×844, 900×1000, 1512×1100 and 1920×1200, and keep menus and the tour card inside the viewport at 1440px and 390px.
- **Do** keep text off chart cells: labels knock their cells out, and values and labels use text colors, never the series color.
- **Do** give every chart a "Table" toggle, a one-sentence summary for screen readers, and the same readout on keyboard focus as on hover.

### Don't:

- **Don't** use `box-shadow` anywhere except the liquid-metal button. That includes shadcn's focus rings, menus, sheets and toasts.
- **Don't** move, scale or bounce anything outside the named motion exceptions. Toasts and cards fade; they never slide.
- **Don't** put white text on green. It is 1.86:1.
- **Don't** tint the liquid-metal rim with Signal Green. It turns the highlights cyan and reads as neon.
- **Don't** use green for body text, headings, container borders, large fills or glows. The hero chart's lit cells are the only green area.
- **Don't** use Soft Mint as a second chart series, a status color as a series color, or an area wash under a trace.
- **Don't** color errors red or successes with green banners. Errors are neutral boxes, and amber means a task is waiting.
- **Don't** use gradients beyond the skeleton sweep and the liquid-metal plate, and no glassmorphism, neon glows or mascot illustrations.
- **Don't** use a text character as an icon, or mix icon sets with heavier strokes.
- **Don't** use radii above 6px, except the hero chart's 32px stepped corners and true circles.
- **Don't** restate visible labels: no desktop top bar, and no helper text that repeats a heading.
- **Don't** put an info icon beside a term or a control. Explain a control with a Hint on the control itself, turn an essential fact into a short visible marker, and cut the rest.
- **Don't** outline cards, tiles, chips, controls or value boxes. Use a tonal fill or spacing; keep hairlines for dividers and terminal pane edges.
- **Don't** add pagination or "load more". Show the first rows and a "View all" link.
- **Don't** open anything on top of the drawer. Confirm and edit inline.
- **Don't** use Lexend Deca or Geist, and don't use Akt inside the dashboard.
- **Don't** frost, blur or lock a page: every dashboard page needs a session. Never call something empty while it is still loading.
- **Don't** collapse an empty chart or table into a heading and one line. Keep its frame and say what is missing.
